| team  | [CollectionRolesInfoTeam](CollectionRolesInfoTeam.md)[] | ❌       | A list of the collection's team roles.  |
| user  | [CollectionRolesInfoUser](CollectionRolesInfoUser.md)[] | ❌       | A list of the collection's user roles.  |
